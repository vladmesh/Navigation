# Human epic owner

Review the agent report, evidence, ADR, `STATUS.md`, and loop counters. Decide whether to change scope, choose an architectural direction, accept a risk, create tasks, or stop the epic.

Record the decision in the ADR or decision log, update `STATUS.md`, and name the next role and exact artifact. Do not ask agents to continue without changing the blocking condition.
